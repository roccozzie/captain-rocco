# Captain Rocco

a flying game for a 3-year-old pilot. you ARE the pilot: touch and drag, and the little red biplane banks and follows your finger around the sky. it cannot crash - the bottom of the sky is a sea of clouds it bounces off of.

- **Live:** (vercel link lands here once the project is created)
- **Fly:** hold or drag anywhere to steer up and down; let go and the plane cruises
- **Stars:** fly into golden stars to fill the star row at the top - fill all 8 for confetti, a sky rainbow, and a celebration
- **Tricks:** tap the plane for a loop-de-loop; swipe forward for a speed boost; double-tap for a wave
- **World:** rainbow rings give the plane a rainbow trail, clouds poof, birds and balloons drift by, and the sky slowly cycles day - sunset - starry night
- **Tech:** one self-contained `index.html` - plane, sky, and every sound (putt-putt engine, chimes, chirps) drawn and synthesized in code, no assets
- no fail states, no timers, no scores, no words to read

Third in the series: [Rocco's Garage](https://github.com/roccozzie/roccos-garage) (tire swapping) and [Wee Woo](https://github.com/roccozzie/wee-woo) (Ozzie's ambulances).
